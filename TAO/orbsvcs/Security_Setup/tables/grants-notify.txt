# Recommended grants for the TAO Notification Service (Phase 3 T2, R34): a
# draft for "ace-tao-setup grants sign --file grants-notify.txt".  Roles
# are given to principals and groups by the role map (roles table).
#
#   observer       reads channels, admins, proxies, QoS and filters
#   participant    also publishes and subscribes (obtains proxies, connects,
#                  pushes and pulls) and changes its own filters and QoS
#   channel-admin  also creates and destroys channels and admins and sets
#                  admin properties
#
# These grants cover the whole service (one domain per service).

grant observer       get
grant participant    get,use,set
grant channel-admin  get,set,manage,use
